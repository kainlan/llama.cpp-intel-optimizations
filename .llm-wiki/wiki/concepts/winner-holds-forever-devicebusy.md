# Winner-holds-forever DEVICE_BUSY

Multi-context failure mode where the first context to begin a tracked graph on a device holds its invocation indefinitely and every other context's claim is refused exactly once with no path to later success; under open P2 adjudication (llama.cpp-u7vj).
