# GGML_SYCL_USE_XMX_GEMM

Routes quantized MUL_MAT through experimental XMX GEMM kernels (measured 5–11x slower); no-op unless the build carries both GGML_SYCL_XMX_GEMM and GGML_SYCL_MMQ_XMX.
